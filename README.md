# Caregiver guide for dementia (Guía para cuidadores)

A free, bilingual (Spanish and English) web guide for family caregivers of people living with dementia, written in plain language and designed to work offline once opened. It is a single static page with no server and no tracking: nothing the caregiver writes leaves their device.

Developed with the team of the Memory and Brain Health Unit, Hospital Dr. Guillermo Rawson, San Juan, Argentina, and given to the families the unit follows. By October 2026 it was in the hands of more than 70 caregivers.

Live page: https://fermarquez88.github.io/kaizenai-cuidadores/

## What it covers

- Understanding dementia and the changes it brings, in everyday words.
- Managing behavioral and psychological symptoms step by step, with a guided plan for difficult moments.
- Looking after the caregiver: a short self-check written for this guide (not a validated scale), small weekly steps, and when to seek professional help.
- Safety at home, routines, communication, legal and care planning, and local help lines for San Juan and Argentina.
- A summary the caregiver can copy and bring to their doctor.

The guide has no research module and collects no data. Any future study with caregivers will be run only after approval by a research ethics committee.

## Principles

- Plain language, reviewed clinically by the unit team.
- No diagnosis and no scoring instruments that require a license. The caregiver self-check uses original questions and is for reflection only; formal assessment of caregiver burden is done in consultation.
- Privacy by design: everything stays in the browser (local storage only, with a button to erase it).
- Accessible: large text, high contrast, read-aloud option, works on low-end phones.

## Status

Version in use since March 2026. Content is updated with feedback from the unit's caregivers' group. Suggestions and corrections are welcome through the issues page.
